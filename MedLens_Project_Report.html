Executive Summary

MedLens is an enterprise-grade clinical diagnostic intelligence platform engineered to ingest, structure, ground, explain, and review fragmented medical documents (multi-page PDFs, lab reports, camera scans, faxes) without LLM hallucinations, diagnostic overreach, or pharmacological prescribing.

Diagnostic reports are typically delivered in disconnected formats with lab-specific reference ranges. MedLens enforces an Auditable, Deterministic-First, Generative-Second Architecture: converting raw inputs into validated Medical JSON and interoperable HL7 FHIR R4 Bundles, establishing bidirectional provenance down to the exact document and page, strictly bounding biomarkers against printed laboratory reference intervals, and providing a physician-in-the-loop review workflow.

2.1 OCR Ambiguity Resolution Framework

Medical faxes and degraded scans often introduce alphanumeric substitution errors. ocrService.ts automatically flags:

  • Digit '1' vs 'I' / 'l': e.g., I1.2 flagged for clinician review before signoff.
  • Digit '0' vs 'O': e.g., O.85 flagged as ambiguous zero.
  • Pipe '|' vs '1': e.g., |4.2 flagged as smudge digit.

2.2 Multi-Model AI Fallback Hierarchy

To prevent service disruptions from API rate limits or network drops, MedLens implements an automated 4-tier fallback:

  1. Tier 1 (Primary): gemini-flash-lite-latest (multimodal structured parsing)
  2. Tier 2 (Preview Fallback): gemini-3-flash-preview
  3. Tier 3 (Secondary Fallback): gemini-3.5-flash-lite
  4. Tier 4 (Offline Engine): Deterministic rule-based clinical parser (100% offline functionality)
