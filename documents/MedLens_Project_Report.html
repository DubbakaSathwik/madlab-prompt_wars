Executive Summary

MedLens is an enterprise-grade clinical diagnostic intelligence platform engineered to ingest, structure, ground, explain, and review fragmented medical documents (multi-page PDFs, laboratory reports, camera scans, faxes) without LLM hallucinations, diagnostic overreach, or pharmacological prescribing.

Diagnostic reports are frequently delivered in disconnected formats with laboratory-specific reference ranges. MedLens enforces an Auditable, Deterministic-First, Generative-Second Architecture: converting raw inputs into validated Medical JSON and interoperable HL7 FHIR R4 Bundles, establishing bidirectional provenance down to the exact document and page, strictly bounding biomarkers against printed laboratory reference intervals, and providing a physician-in-the-loop review workflow.

3. The OCR Ambiguity Resolution Framework

Diagnostic laboratory reports delivered via facsimile or mobile phone cameras frequently suffer from character degradation, noise artifacts, and optical smudges. The MedLens OCR Ambiguity Engine (src/services/ocrService.ts) enforces a multi-pass heuristic detection matrix to prevent smudged characters from corrupting medical decisions.

Flowchart 2: OCR Smudge & Ambiguity Detection Architecture

3.1 Ambiguity Detection Rules & Classification Matrix

Smudge Archetype Detected Example Heuristic Resolution Clinical Safety Action
Digit '1' vs 'I' / 'l' Hemoglobin: I1.2 g/dL Identifies leading capital I preceding decimal point. Flags metric as NEEDS_REVIEW; shows original document crop for verification.
Digit '0' vs 'O' Total Bilirubin: O.85 mg/dL Detects capital O before decimal point in numerical column. Flags ambiguous_zero; pre-populates corrected candidate 0.85 for confirmation.
Vertical Smudge '|' WBC: |4.2 10^3/uL Detects non-alphanumeric pipe separator prepending integer. Neutralizes artifact smudge; flags for clinician verification.
Missing Decimal Point Hemoglobin: 112 g/dL Value deviates by 10x from biological plausibility table. High-priority alert: flags probable missing decimal point (11.2).

4. The AI Multi-Tier Fallback Hierarchy & Safety Governance

To guarantee 100% operational uptime, zero user dead ends, and strict medical governance, MedLens implements an automated 4-tier model fallback hierarchy coupled with deterministic pre-inference safety filters.

Flowchart 3: AI Multi-Tier Fallback Hierarchy & Safety Governance

4.1 Priority 1 Biomarker-First Evaluation Architecture

Previous conversational LLMs often return generic lists of all out-of-range metrics when asked about a specific test. In src/services/aiService.ts, MedLens implements Biomarker-First Routing:

  1. Target Isolation: If the prompt mentions a specific biomarker (e.g., Triglycerides), the engine immediately isolates that record.
  2. Document Reference Interval Comparison: Compares the exact value (e.g., 229 mg/dL) against the laboratory's printed interval (< 150 mg/dL).
  3. Biological Context Without Diagnosis: Explains what Triglycerides are (blood lipids) and why elevated levels warrant discussion with a doctor, without formulating cardiovascular disease diagnoses or prescribing statins.

Laboratories calibrate their normal reference intervals to specific analytical machinery and reagent lots. MedLens strictly evaluates extracted values against the document's printed interval:

Flowchart 4: Laboratory Reference Range Bounding Engine

5.1 Standards Interoperability (HL7 FHIR R4)

MedLens features a native FHIR generation engine (src/services/fhirService.ts) that serializes structured records into standard HL7 FHIR Release 4 JSON Bundles:

  • Bundle (type: collection or document)
  • Patient (identifiers, name, age, sex)
  • DiagnosticReport (status: final, category: LAB, issuance timestamp)
  • Observation (LOINC codes, numerical value, unit, reference interval min/max, interpretation flags)
